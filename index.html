English

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the OFFROAD JANKEN application ("the App") handles user information.

2. Information Collection

The App does not collect personal information. Rock-paper-scissors results and prediction-related data entered in the App are processed and stored locally on the user's device and are not transmitted to external servers.

3. Purpose of Use

Data stored on the device is used solely to provide the App's functionality.

4. Disclosure to Third Parties

The App does not provide users' personal information to third parties.

5. Data Storage and Deletion

App data is stored on the user's device. Users may delete data using any deletion feature provided by the App or through Android's app data management settings.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. Any changes will be posted on this page.
